About Us

Connecting Filipino workers with opportunities abroad.

Who We Are

Pinoy Horizon is an overseas employment platform designed to help Filipino workers discover job opportunities in different countries.

Our goal is to make searching for overseas employment easier, clearer, and more organized for applicants.

Our Mission

Our mission is to connect Filipino job seekers with employment opportunities abroad while providing a simple and user-friendly experience.

Our Vision

We envision a platform where Filipino workers can easily explore international career opportunities and make informed decisions about their future.
